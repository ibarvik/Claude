How to read this

The source lists include anyone in the global top 100,000 by composite score c or in the top 2% of their main subfield, among authors with at least five papers. The dataset gives each author one country, taken from their most recent Scopus affiliation, and separately counts the countries of their career papers. The groups use both.

In Czechia
Most recent affiliation is in the Czech Republic. Includes foreign researchers now working there.
Czech career, abroad
Most recent affiliation is abroad, but the Czech Republic is the most frequent country on their career papers. Some are Czech researchers who moved; some are Czech-based people whose latest Scopus affiliation is a foreign co-affiliation.
≥10% Czech papers
Most recent affiliation is abroad, the Czech Republic is not their top country, but it accounts for at least 10% of their career papers. Often long-term collaborators or researchers who spent part of their career in Czechia.

c
Composite indicator combining total citations, h-index, co-authorship-adjusted hm-index and citations to single, first and last-author papers.
Subfield top %
Rank within the author's main subfield divided by the number of authors in that subfield.
